Data Release

Plans for the public release of GLIMPSE imaging, catalogs, and lens models, and how to cite the survey once you use them.

The first public data release is in preparation. The products listed below are the planned contents of the release, drawn from commitments made in the accepted JWST proposal, plus one product described only by the survey overview paper. Download links will be added to this page when the Zenodo record is published. No release date is set.

Related spectroscopy

JWST/NIRSpec multi-object spectroscopy of this field was taken separately by the GLIMPSE-D DDT program (DD 9223) and is public on MAST. It is not part of this imaging data release and is not listed in the products table above.

Release history

Release Planned contents Status
DR1 Drizzled multi-filter NIRCam mosaics. In preparation
DR2 ICL and BCG subtracted NIRCam mosaics (GALFIT, following Shipley et al. 2018). In preparation
DR3 PSF-matched multi-wavelength photometric catalogs with EAZY, Bagpipes, and BEAGLE photometric redshifts and SED fits, morphology, and gravitational lens-model deflection, convergence, and shear maps with uncertainties. In preparation
Timing and contents per the accepted proposal's data-management plan: DR1 at 3 months after observation, DR2 at 6 months, DR3 at 9 months. All deliverables were committed to MAST. HST mosaics of the field are not part of this proposal schedule; see the Data products table above for their separate provenance.

Suggested acknowledgement

This is a suggested wording, not an official requirement:

This work is based on observations made with the NASA/ESA/CSA James Webb Space Telescope, associated with JWST GO program 3293 (GLIMPSE).

Data policy

GLIMPSE has an exclusive access period of zero months, so all GLIMPSE observations are public as soon as they are taken.

About GLIMPSE

GLIMPSE is JWST GO program 3293, an ultra-deep NIRCam imaging survey of the lensing cluster Abell S1063. Co-PIs: Hakim Atek (Institut d'Astrophysique de Paris) and John Chisholm (University of Texas at Austin).
