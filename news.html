Press coverage

2025-05-27 · ESA/Webb

A glimpse of the distant past

ESA/Webb's Picture of the Month for May 2025 presents the deep NIRCam mosaic of the GLIMPSE field on Abell S1063, describing it as roughly 120 hours of observing time and the deepest single-target Webb observation to date.

Credit: ESA/Webb, NASA & CSA, H. Atek, M. Zamani (ESA/Webb); Acknowledgement: R. Endsley

CNRS press release (in French; the headline translates as "The James Webb Space Telescope reveals the deepest image of the Universe") announcing the same GLIMPSE NIRCam mosaic covered in the ESA/Webb release above.

About GLIMPSE

GLIMPSE is JWST GO program 3293, an ultra-deep NIRCam imaging survey of the lensing cluster Abell S1063. Co-PIs: Hakim Atek (CNRS / Institut d'Astrophysique de Paris) and John Chisholm (University of Texas at Austin).
