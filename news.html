News and press

Press and image releases connected to GLIMPSE, JWST GO program 3293. Each item below was fetched and checked against its own primary source page before being listed here.

Press coverage

2025-05-27 · ESA/Webb

A glimpse of the distant past

ESA/Webb's Picture of the Month for May 2025 presents the deep NIRCam mosaic of the GLIMPSE field on Abell S1063, describing it as roughly 120 hours of observing time and the deepest single-target Webb observation to date.

Credit: ESA/Webb, NASA & CSA, H. Atek, M. Zamani (ESA/Webb); Acknowledgement: R. Endsley

2025-05-27 · CNRS and Sorbonne Université

Le télescope spatial James Webb dévoile l'image la plus profonde de l'Univers

CNRS press release, in French, announcing the same GLIMPSE NIRCam mosaic covered in the ESA/Webb release above. The headline translates as "The James Webb Space Telescope reveals the deepest image of the Universe". Sorbonne Université issued the release under the same headline.

2026-06-10 · ESA/Webb

Webb finds strongest evidence yet for “black hole stars”

ESA/Webb news release built around the GLIMPSE NIRCam view of Abell S1063, highlighting the source GLIMPSE-17775 within the field.

Credit: NASA, ESA, CSA, V. Kokorev (University of Texas at Austin), A. Pagan (STScI)

About GLIMPSE

GLIMPSE is JWST GO program 3293, an ultra-deep NIRCam imaging survey of the lensing cluster Abell S1063. Co-PIs: Hakim Atek (Institut d'Astrophysique de Paris) and John Chisholm (University of Texas at Austin).
